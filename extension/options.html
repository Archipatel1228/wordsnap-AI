Privacy notice

What leaves your browser, and when:

  • Only the single word you look up (plus your chosen explanation level) is sent to the WordSnap lookup service to fetch its dictionary entry and AI explanation. Page content, URLs and surrounding text are never sent.
  • Words you save are written to your WordSnap account so vocabulary, favourites and streaks stay in sync.
  • Your sign-in session is mirrored from the WordSnap website into memory-only storage that is cleared when the browser closes. It is never written to disk and never shared with the pages you visit.
  • Settings on this page stay on this device (local storage only — not synced to any account).
  • No analytics, no tracking, no page scraping. The extension only requests access to the WordSnap lookup endpoint and your WordSnap database.
